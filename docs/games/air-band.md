# Air Band (`air-band`)

**Inspired by:** Rock Band, Guitar Hero
**Wave:** Later · **Modes:** Teams, Solo battles · **Players:** 2-8 · **Length:** 15 min · **Effort:** High
**Phone features:** `custom:air-band/drums`, `custom:air-band/guitar`, `custom:air-band/bass`, haptics
**Music source:** file only (needs stems and chart.json) - see architecture section 12

## Pitch
Your phones become instruments. Tap drums, strum guitar and hold bass notes to the real song as notes fly down the TV.

## Intro and presentation
- **Title sequence:** stage pyro bursts, an amp stack rises, and "AIR BAND" is struck like a power chord - letters shake with the distortion.
- **Signature moment:** "Band Meter" in the corner and a crowd that cheers or boos live with your accuracy; hitting a 20-note streak triggers "STAR POWER" with the screen going gold.

## How to Play lines
- Pick your instrument: drums, guitar or bass.
- Notes fly down your lane on the TV.
- Tap, strum or hold right on the line.
- Keep the Band Meter high or the crowd will boo you off!

## Flow
1. **BAND** - Players pick roles (`choices`), max 4 per band.
2. **SONG** - Only songs with `chart.json`.
3. **PLAY** - TV: one note highway per player (PixiJS), song playing. Drums phone: 3 big pads. Guitar: 3 fret buttons held with the thumb + swipe anywhere to strum, tilt up for star power. Bass: 2 long pads, hold for long notes. Each input sends `{ lane, clientTime }`; the server judges against the chart: Perfect +/- 50 ms, Good +/- 100 ms, else miss.
4. **RESULT** - Accuracy, streaks, band rank. `ctx.endRound()`.

## Scoring
- Perfect 100, Good 50, streak multiplier x2 at 10, x3 at 20, x4 at 30.

## Content
`content/songs/<id>/chart.json` `{ drums: [{ t, lane }], guitar: [...], bass: [...] }` generated by `tools/song-tools/chart.ts` from Demucs stems via onset detection, thinned for difficulty.
