# Dance Off (`dance-off`)

**Inspired by:** Just Dance, So You Think You Can Dance
**Wave:** 3 · **Modes:** Solo, Teams · **Players:** 1-16 · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Effort:** Medium
**Phone features:** `motion` (hand-held mode), `pose` (camera mode), haptics
**Music source:** YouTube or file - see architecture section 12

## Pitch
Copy the dancer on the TV while your phone scores every move - hold it in your hand, or prop it up and let the camera track your whole body.

## Intro and presentation
- **Title sequence:** a disco floor of coloured tiles lights up in a wave, a mirror ball drops and spins, and "DANCE OFF" slides in with a stutter-step animation to an original four-on-the-floor beat.
- **Signature moments:** move icons glide up from the bottom of the TV a beat early; every scored move pops a word over the player's avatar (PERFECT, GOOD, OK, MISS) with matching colour; 10-move streaks make the floor tiles under that avatar light up.

## How to Play lines
- Hold your phone in your right hand (or prop it up for camera mode).
- Copy the dancer on the TV - moves are shown a beat early.
- Every move is scored. Keep your streak going!

## Flow
1. **CALIBRATE** - Hand-held: `motion` "Wave your arm side to side for 5 seconds" (baseline energy). Camera: `pose` "Step back until your whole body fits the outline".
2. **SONG** - Room votes on 3 songs (any song in the library with BPM; YouTube or file).
3. **DANCE** - TV: the dancer (an avatar-style figure animated from the routine's key poses with tweening), move cue strip, live leaderboard of avatars bouncing. Phones: `motion` or `pose` running the routine; each move result is sent as `{ moveIndex, grade }` (scored on the phone, verified for plausibility on the server: grades must arrive within 400 ms of the move window).
4. **RESULT** - Scores, best streak, "Dancer of the round". `ctx.endRound()` per song.

## Scoring
- Perfect 100, Good 60, OK 30, Miss 0. Streak bonus +5 per move in a streak over 5.
- Teams: sum of members / members (average) so team size doesn't matter.

## Content
- `content/dance-off/moves.json`: each move `{ id, name, icon, beats, accelSignature: { axis: 'x'|'y'|'z'|'mix', direction: [x,y,z], minEnergy }, keyPose: Landmarks33 }` (24 moves: punch up, wave left, wave right, roll arms, clap, jump, disco point, spin, shimmy, sprinkler, robot arms, hands on hips).
- `content/dance-off/routines/<songId>.json` generated by `tools/song-tools/routine.ts` from BPM and difficulty (one move every 2 beats at difficulty 1, every beat at 3), or hand-authored.
- Key poses are recorded with the `/dev/pose-recorder` page.

## Scoring maths
- Hand-held: in the window [beat - 150 ms, beat + 250 ms] compute the mean acceleration vector after removing gravity; grade on cosine similarity with `direction` (> 0.8 Perfect, > 0.6 Good, > 0.35 OK) only if energy > `minEnergy` x calibration factor.
- Camera: at the beat, compute angles for 8 joints (elbows, shoulders, hips, knees) and compare with the key pose: mean absolute angle difference < 15 degrees Perfect, < 25 Good, < 40 OK.

## Done when
- Grading functions are unit-tested with recorded fixture data in `tools/bots/fixtures/dance/`.
- Results arriving outside the window are rejected.
- Calibration scales the energy threshold.
